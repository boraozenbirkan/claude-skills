## Register

| Operator | Vocabulary | Recommendations | Explain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-technical | Outcomes and screens; trade off in money, time, and risk | Strong: one recommendation, consequence of the alternative in a clause | Only unavoidable terms, in one line |
| Technical, different domain | Technical terms freely; define the **domain** terms | Strong on domain shape, open on implementation | Domain concepts, not engineering ones |
| Domain-experienced | Full technical and domain vocabulary | Offer the trade-off; they will pick | Nothing by default |

Note that depth is per project, not per career. A distributed-systems veteran building their first
storefront is *technical, different domain*: give them the retail vocabulary and take the
infrastructure as read.

Whatever the register, always state a recommendation. Overruling one costs a word; making the
decision from scratch costs a meeting.
